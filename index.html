Frequently Asked Questions

Answers & Clarifications

01 What is Lumen?

Lumen is a healthcare-navigation platform that helps users discover healthcare facilities, explore appointment options, manage administrative requirements and keep track of follow-up steps.

02 How does Lumen recommend healthcare facilities?

Lumen considers factors such as requested services, location, language preferences, accessibility requirements and appointment information to identify potentially suitable facilities. It also provides transparent explanations.

03 Can I book appointments through Lumen?

Users can explore available appointment slots and submit booking requests through the platform. In this prototype, bookings are simulated demonstration records.

04 Does Lumen provide medical advice or diagnosis?

No. Lumen is strictly an administrative healthcare-navigation platform. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatments, or make clinical decisions.

05 How does Lumen handle sudden API contract changes?

Lumen isolates external data contracts behind an adapter normalization layer. When an external partner API mutates its payload (nested structures, renamed keys, changed units), the adapter validates, translates, and normalizes into canonical models without crashing the core app.
